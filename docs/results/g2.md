# Gate G2 — accuracy: PASS (1.000 on unseen problems)

Headline, with its config: **L1 eval_heldout, 2,000 problems, exact-match 1.000** — g2-diffusion (10M, 20k steps, frozen corpus), steps = masked count, ordering = confidence, temperature 0, seed 0. The target was ≥0.90.

The heldout split is a deterministic md5-based 10% of the instance space: strings the model has *never trained on*, drawn from the same digit distribution it knows. That's the standard generalization claim. It saturates — and the step count barely matters: the ablation grid hit 1.000 at 8, 4, and even 2 denoising steps, for all four orderings.

## The full L1 picture

| model | training data | heldout (unseen problems) | perturbed (censored digits) |
|---|---|---|---|
| diffusion 10M | frozen, ~370 epochs | **1.000** | **0.258** |
| diffusion 10M | fresh, never repeats | 1.000 | 0.190 |
| ar twin 10M | frozen, ~370 epochs | 0.999 | 0.092 |

Well-formed rate: 1.000 everywhere, every model, every split. Nothing here ever produced gibberish — formatting is learned unconditionally; arithmetic is what varies.

## Three findings

1. **Diffusion beats its AR twin ~3× on digit extrapolation** (0.258 vs 0.092). The perturbed split holds out operand-final digits 8/9 from training entirely, so it asks for extrapolation to digit patterns never seen in those positions. Both models degrade hard — this is a known open problem, and we said up front we wouldn't claim it — but at matched trunk, data, and compute, the masked-diffusion objective degrades three times more gracefully. One seed, one size, one task family: a local result, not a law. Still the single most interesting number this project produced.

2. **Frozen re-epoching beat fresh data for diffusion** on the stress split (0.258 vs 0.190, heldout identical). The research literature fights about whether diffusion's multi-epoch advantage is diffusion-specific or generic regularization; this doesn't settle anything, but the direction — heavy reuse of a fixed corpus not hurting, maybe helping — matches the data-constrained story the build bet on.

3. **Ordering doesn't matter when the model is sure, and confidence isn't trustworthy when it isn't.** On heldout (easy), all orderings tie at 1.000. On the earlier stress ablation (hard), random beat confidence (0.112 vs 0.084 at 8 steps). Confidence-guided unmasking needs calibration a 10M model doesn't have — measured locally, as the dossier predicted from 7-8B-scale literature.

## Eval hygiene notes

Every number above: exact-match via the external checker, full 2,000-row splits, config stamped in `docs/results/results.md` (append-only ledger) and per-run JSON. The `eval` split (in-distribution, may overlap train) stays out of headline use — it reads 1.000 for everything and means only "memorization + format complete." L1's instance space is small (~20k strings); saturating heldout means the model effectively learned the full addition/subtraction table it was shown plus the 10% it wasn't. L2/L3 (multiplication, carries, precedence) are training now and get their own rows.

## Verdict

G2 passes at 1.000 against a 0.90 target. Claims stay scoped: unseen problems within the trained digit distribution — yes; censored-digit extrapolation — 0.258 and honestly labeled; word problems and longer numbers — never claimed.
